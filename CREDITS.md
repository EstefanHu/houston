## 3D models

All rockets are placeholder geometry described in their data files and built by
`js/scene/model.ts`. No third-party models are used.

## Rocket names and facts

Saturn V, Apollo, Falcon 9, Falcon Heavy, Black Brant and other names are used to identify real
vehicles for educational purposes. They're the property of their respective owners, and
Houston isn't affiliated with or endorsed by NASA, SpaceX or Magellan Aerospace. Houston-1 is
fictional.

Figures and timings come from these sources (also listed in each rocket's data file and in the
app's About section):

- **Saturn V / Apollo 11:** [Saturn V Launch Vehicle Flight Evaluation Report AS-506](https://ibiblio.org/apollo/Documents/lvfea-AS506-Apollo11.pdf); [Apollo 11 Flight Journal](https://www.apollojournals.org/afj/ap11fj/01launch.html)
- **Falcon 9:** [Spaceflight Now, CRS-12 timeline](https://spaceflightnow.com/2017/08/13/timeline-for-spacexs-next-station-cargo-launch/); [Spaceflight Now, first landing at Cape Canaveral](https://spaceflightnow.com/2015/12/19/rocket-landing-at-cape-canaveral-planned-after-sundays-spacex-launch/)
- **Falcon Heavy:** [Spaceflight Now, second-flight timeline](https://spaceflightnow.com/2019/04/10/launch-timeline-for-falcon-heavys-second-flight/)
- **Black Brant IX:** [NASA Sounding Rockets Program](https://www.nasa.gov/soundingrockets/vehicles); [Designation Systems: Black Brant](https://designation-systems.net/dusrm/app4/blackbrant.html)
